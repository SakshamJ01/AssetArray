# AssetArray Product Terminology Glossary

**Version:** 3.3.1  
**Category:** Standard Governance Vocabulary

This glossary establishes the canonical, defensible product terminology across AssetArray UI screens, PDF reports, client statements, documentation, and AI prompts.

---

### Core Governance & Operating Terms

1. **Advisor Command Center**
   - *Definition:* The daily mission-control workspace for wealth advisors, synthesising proactive priority queues, governance alerts, real-time client indicators, and action orchestration into a unified interface.
   - *Replaces:* "Fiduciary Command Center", "Fiduciary OS".

2. **Advisor Governance & Decision Support**
   - *Definition:* The systematic operational framework that couples deterministic financial intelligence engines with human-in-the-loop advisor documentation and mandate tracking.
   - *Replaces:* "Fiduciary Compliance Engine", "Automated Fiduciary Management".

3. **Advisor Decision Journal**
   - *Definition:* An evidence-backed operational log recording advisor issues, observed metrics, deterministic data provenance, rationale, follow-ups, and review timestamps.
   - *Replaces:* "Fiduciary Decision Journal".

4. **Grounded AI Advisor Brief**
   - *Definition:* A high-signal daily briefing generated using deterministic analytical models, zero-hallucination factual guardrails, and sanitized portfolio context.
   - *Replaces:* "Daily Fiduciary Brief", "Automated Advisor AI".

---

### Analytical & Regulatory Methodology Terms

5. **GIPS-Aligned Performance Methodology**
   - *Definition:* Performance measurement algorithms (specifically daily subperiod Time-Weighted Return and Modified Dietz external cash-flow treatment) designed around concepts in the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS®).
   - *Important:* AssetArray is software, not a claiming firm. Use of GIPS-aligned methodology does not certify or imply that AssetArray or its users are GIPS-compliant.

6. **Estimated Tax Impact**
   - *Definition:* Algorithmic projections of capital gains tax liabilities, harvestable losses, and net tax implications calculated using statutory rules (e.g., India Finance Act 2024 AY 2026-27 or US IRS Section 1091 wash-sale guidelines).
   - *Replaces:* "Tax Shield", "Guaranteed Tax Savings", "Tax Optimization Guarantee".

7. **Potential Tax Opportunity**
   - *Definition:* Quantified unrealized loss lots eligible for offset against realized gains or statutory exemptions within allowable holding thresholds.
   - *Replaces:* "Optimal Tax Harvest", "Guaranteed Tax Shield".

8. **DPDP-Aligned Privacy Controls**
   - *Definition:* Architectural data-protection features—including client-side pseudonymization, zero-knowledge encryption, and local-first vector memory—designed in alignment with the data minimization principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).
   - *Replaces:* "DPDP Compliant", "DPDP Certified".

9. **Suitability-Support Tooling**
   - *Definition:* Quantitative risk profiling, asset-class tolerance bands, and portfolio rebalancing simulators designed to assist Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) in fulfilling SEBI suitability obligations.
   - *Replaces:* "SEBI Compliant Software", "SEBI Certified Platform".

10. **Institutional-Style Analytics**
    - *Definition:* Rigorous quantitative methodologies—including multi-factor Brinson-Fachler attribution, parametric and Monte Carlo Value-at-Risk, and daily subperiod TWR—typically utilized by institutional asset managers.
    - *Replaces:* "Institutionally Certified Platform", "Institutional Compliance".
