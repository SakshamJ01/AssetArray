## 2. What is NEVER Paywalled
1. Core Portfolio Valuation & Holding Calculations (Never lock an advisor out of their client data).
2. Data Export (Advisors always retain 100% ownership of their data with instant CSV/JSON exports).
3. Basic Risk & Capital Gains calculations.

---

## 3. Cost-Aware Unit Economics
- **AI Inference Costs**: Using Gemini 2.5 Flash and caching prompt embeddings limits average AI server cost to less than **₹30 ($0.35) per advisor per month**.
- **Data Feed Costs**: Leveraging official open public data (AMFI, RBI, open quotes) keeps data feed overhead at **₹0 for free tiers**.
- **Gross Margins**: Estimated SaaS gross margin exceeds **88%** at scale.
