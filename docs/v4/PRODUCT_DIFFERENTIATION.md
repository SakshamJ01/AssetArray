## 2. Four Defensible Product Differentiators

### Differentiator 1: Indian-First Tax-Aware Portfolio Rebalancing (Sec 70/74)
- *The Edge*: While global tools (Addepar/Orion) are tax-blind in India and local tools simply calculate historical capital gains, AssetArray 4.0 features a forward-looking **Tax Lot Optimization Engine**. It minimizes STCG/LTCG during rebalancing by picking specific tax lots, offsetting STCL against LTCG, and maximizing the annual ₹1.25L exemption.

### Differentiator 2: Grounded Financial AI Sits Above Deterministic Engines
- *The Edge*: Unlike "AI wrapper" apps that feed random PDF text to an LLM, AssetArray 4.0's Copilot pulls strictly from deterministic financial calculations (XIRR, VaR, tax lots). It produces zero hallucinations and provides clickable provenance citations for every claim.

### Differentiator 3: The Dedicated 3-Stage Client Meeting Workspace
- *The Edge*: Slashes advisor pre-meeting prep from 45 minutes to 3 minutes. Provides an instant 1-page condensed brief, switches to a client-safe interactive simulation canvas, and auto-drafts post-meeting follow-ups in 90 seconds.

### Differentiator 4: Multi-Entity Household Family Office Modeling
- *The Edge*: Seamlessly aggregates wealth across Husband, Wife, HUF entities, family trusts, and private holding companies with automated inter-entity loan tracking and cross-portfolio concentration detection.
