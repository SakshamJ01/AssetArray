# AssetArray V4.0 — Phase 4: Grounded AI Layer

## 1. Overview & AI Rule of Law
In AssetArray V4.0, Artificial Intelligence operates strictly as an **Assistance and Interpretation Layer**, seated directly above verified deterministic foundations:

```
PHASE 1: Multi-Tenancy & RBAC Foundation
  ↓
PHASE 2: Deterministic Financial Engines (TWR, XIRR, Tax Lots, Corridors, Rebalancing)
  ↓
PHASE 3: Advisor Operating Workflow (Tasks, Decisions, Meeting Workspace, Activity)
  ↓
PHASE 4: Grounded AI Copilot (Explain, Summarize, Research, Draft, Challenge)
```

## 2. Core Rule of Law
- **Zero Autonomous Financial Actions**: AI cannot execute broker trades, mutate client records, adjust target allocations, or approve decisions.
- **Zero Hallucinated Numbers**: Deterministic engines supply all valuations, tax calculations, risk metrics, and rebalance math.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**: Consequential outputs (draft communications, meeting briefs, rebalance explanations) require explicit human advisor review.
- **Free-First Provider Routing**: Prioritizes local Ollama and free cloud APIs with deterministic rule-based fallbacks.
